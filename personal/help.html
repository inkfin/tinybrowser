Keys & commands

Space opens the menu. Use j/k or arrows to select, Enter to run, Escape/q to cancel. Right click opens the same actions with extra choices for a link, image or selection.

Ctrl-P asks for a command name, then shows matching actions. Use the : bar for the commands below.

Mouse

Click a link to follow it. Middle click a link to open it in a background tab; gt switches to the next tab. Use the wheel to scroll, drag to select text, then y or right click → Copy selected text. Click a menu entry to run it, or click outside to cancel.

Shift-drag uses your terminal's own selection in terminals that support it. If mouse events are unavailable, all commands remain available from the keyboard.

Plain words in the URL bar become a web search. Explicit URLs, s:/w: shortcuts and paths such as ./notes.html are kept as entered.

Website JS and cookies start disabled. :javascript and :cookies toggle them for the current page; the menu shows their state. Reader styling hides no content; gr restores website CSS. Reload and style changes retain your reading position.

:x opens X in your system browser. :external opens the current web page there, and :external-link opens the link under the cursor. These actions support http(s) URLs.

Upgrades keep your keybindings. If gx/gX are not in your existing profile, use :external/:external-link or add the shortcuts to your config.

Small terminals scroll the menu. Images depend on Kitty or Sixel support; direct terminal use works best.
